Optional layer

Physical presence, only when it is needed

The protest works completely without anyone leaving their home. Sometimes an event needs a small physical presence — a delegation, a handover, a briefing. That is a separate layer with its own steps: request, verify, schedule. It never becomes a requirement, and it never becomes a crowd.

Step 1
Request
Any participant may request it. No obligation either way.
Step 2
Verify
Identity and intent are verified before anything is scheduled.
Step 3
Select
People are drawn from different areas, not one dense city.
Step 4
Schedule
A short, specific time window under the same protocol.

Why verification comes first

Digital participation needs no identity. A physical gathering does — for safety, not surveillance.

  • Verification confirms a real person is making the request, and that the intent is peaceful.
  • Verification data is held separately from protest counts and is never shown in a KPI.
  • Nobody is added to a physical schedule without their own explicit consent.
  • Declining has no effect on digital participation. Your join stays counted.
Nothing here organises a march, blockade or occupation. Protocol article 7 stands: local law always applies, and the platform is never used to coordinate an obstruction.

How a group is selected

Rule Reason
Maximum group size is small and capped A crowd is not the point; a credible, calm delegation is.
Participants come from different areas and countries No single neighbourhood or city bears the burden or the risk.
Nobody attends without their own confirmed consent Accepting the digital protocol is not accepting a physical ask.
Every scheduled group signs the same protocol One standard, regardless of where the group meets.
A scheduled event can be cancelled at any time Safety outranks attendance, always.
